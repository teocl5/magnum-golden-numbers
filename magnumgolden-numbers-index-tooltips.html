How to read this analysis

Actual vs expected: Actual appearances are counted from the collected historical results. Expected λ is the average count under an equal-probability model.

Individual rarity: asks how unusual the observed count would be for this particular number considered on its own.

Whole-field rarity: accounts for examining all 10,000 possible 4D numbers. With so many numbers, some individually unusual-looking results can occur naturally.

Important: These measures describe historical frequency. They do not increase or decrease a number’s probability in a future independent draw.
